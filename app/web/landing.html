What holds the sheet up

Three constraints, enforced in the product.

Vocabulary 40

A closed vocabulary of 40 terms across play family, formation, motion, coverage and personnel. Nothing outside the list can be written to a play.

Tagging 2 GAs

Two blind taggers are required per play. Field and term agreement must reach 80% on calibration, or the term is removed from the vocabulary.

Freeze Thursday

The coordinator freezes the Thursday sheet. Once frozen, decision rules and player-look evidence become immutable.

Public play-by-play arrives as source rows and stays that way. It is never promoted into a formation, coverage, motion or play family on its own — a tendency exists only where two humans agreed on the film.

What this is not

The boundary is part of the product.

  • Not Hudl
  • Not PFF
  • Not a data API
  • No raw CFBD or nflverse export

Film stays with the customer. FIELDMIND holds authorized links and normalized telestration coordinates, not video. There is no public data portal, no feed redistribution, and no automated inference of coverage, concept, personnel, formation, motion or play family from public play-by-play.
